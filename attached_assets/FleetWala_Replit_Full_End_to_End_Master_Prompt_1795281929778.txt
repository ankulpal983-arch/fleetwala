ORIGINAL PRD BUSINESS RULES THAT MUST REMAIN INTACT
1. FleetWala serves India-focused last-mile logistics/delivery hiring, not a general job portal. Relevant roles include delivery, pickup/drop, warehouse and hub.
2. Worker applies for free; enforce MAXIMUM 20 applications per worker per Asia/Kolkata local calendar day. 20th is permitted, 21st is refused, even with simultaneous requests, retries or different browser tabs. Count policy for withdrawn applications needs documented owner decision; safe default: all successfully created applications count for that calendar day.
3. LIVE job visibility: show only published, approved, non-expired, non-paused, non-closed jobs. Server must recheck job eligibility at moment of applying. New/untrusted employer jobs require admin review; trust-based auto-approval, if implemented, requires an explicit audited rule.
4. Nearby jobs default to 15 km. Provide radius controls, search and filters. GPS access is consent-based; manual city/area/pincode fallback works without GPS. If exact distance cannot be determined, clearly label approximate/city-based matching and do not fake kilometers.
5. Worker discoverability is OFF by default. Only explicit opt-in makes anonymized worker preview eligible for employer candidate discovery. Toggling off must immediately suppress new discovery, subject to separate policy for prior applications/authorized conversations.
6. Employer preview before payment/contact authorization must exclude name, photo, phone, WhatsApp, email, precise address, and any other identifying fields. A worker applying to a job does NOT automatically reveal their phone number.
7. Aadhaar is NOT a standard profile requirement; optional documents must be private. Driving licence is optional for worker onboarding; an individual job may require it.
8. In-app chat is text-only, authorized AFTER shortlisting or explicit approved authorization. No voice/video calling, no AI matching/AI assistant, no salary disbursement, no escrow, no payroll or attendance in initial scope.
9. Employer account types: Company, Vendor/DSP, Recruitment Agency, Recruiter (recruiter may be a staff member tied to employer organization; clarify data taxonomy without creating uncontrolled access).
10. Future monetization as stated in PRD: INR 99 per job posting, INR 10 per candidate contact unlock; never charge the same employer/worker pair twice for that unlock. Use a configurable pricing table and feature flag; no collection of real money until gateway selection, legal/business approval and successful tests.
11. Failed, pending, forged, mismatched or duplicate/replayed payments MUST NEVER grant a job publishing entitlement or reveal worker contact. Admin review before refund/replacement for invalid-contact disputes.
12. English UI is the original baseline; Hindi + English is an OPEN decision. Design localization-ready strings, do not claim Hindi is approved by PRD.
13. Worker UX includes splash/onboarding, OTP screens, profile setup/edit, jobs feed, search/filters, job details, save/unsave, application confirmation, My Applications/status timeline, notifications, privacy controls, settings, support and logout.
14. Every screen needs loading, empty, error, offline/retry and permission-denied states. Work on Indian mobile networks; paginate and optimize.

USER ROLES, TENANCY AND ACCESS CONTROL
- Anonymous visitor: marketing/landing and access to sign-up/log-in; NO access to employer-only job browser, restricted candidate previews or private data. Decide public job visibility consistently with original PRD and document it. Worker after authentication can see approved LIVE jobs.
